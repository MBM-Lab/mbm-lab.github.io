Our former student Cecilia Torboli, who completed her Master's degree in October 2025 and is now a PhD student at the University of Udine on a project carried out in collaboration with the CRO of Aviano, has received a Best Poster Award (joint winner) at ECMTB 2026 (the European Conference on Mathematical and Theoretical Biology), which this year was held as a joint meeting of the European Society for Mathematical and Theoretical Biology (ESMTB) and the Society for Mathematical Biology (SMB).

The award-winning poster presented the results of Cecilia's Master's thesis, carried out at the University of Trento under the supervision of Cinzia Soresina and Jan Elias (Boehringer Ingelheim).

Congratulations, Cecilia!

For more information please visit this [link](https://ecmtb2026.org/).
